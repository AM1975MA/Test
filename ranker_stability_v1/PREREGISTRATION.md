# ETF_TRADER_RANKER_STABILITY_V1 — preregistration

## Purpose
Replace only the unstable Compact21 ranking learner identified by perturbation forensics. Do not change features, labels, annual cutoff logic, Tail model, macro rule, Hybrid24, risk or execution.

## Frozen evidence motivating this line
- Same raw snapshot is computationally deterministic.
- Cross-snapshot ppm perturbations leave inference features almost unchanged but materially alter Compact21 after refitting.
- Titanium component ablation localizes the dominant amplification to Compact21; Tail is almost invariant and macro boost is inactive in the audited period.
- Full feature Q4 rounding reduces CAGR span but does not solve Top1 instability and reduces mean CAGR.
- Dynamic single-thread lookahead closure passes; no operational lookahead was found in the canonical replay.

## Phase A — ranker benchmark
Frozen years: 2017, 2020, 2023, 2026.
Frozen inputs: TI_COMPACT.parquet from the three frozen Yahoo repeat snapshots generated by forensic run 37150436612.
Training eligibility is unchanged:
signal_date < Jan 1 of test year AND exit_date_21 < Jan 1 of test year AND >=30 valid F2D features.

Algorithms, one preregistered recipe each:
1. XGB_CANONICAL — exact current 3-seed XGBRanker baseline.
2. LGBM_XENDCG — LightGBM rank_xendcg, 3 fixed seeds.
3. LGBM_LAMBDARANK — LightGBM lambdarank, 3 fixed seeds.
4. CAT_YETI — CatBoostRanker YetiRankPairwise, 3 fixed seeds.
5. RIDGE_POINTWISE — deterministic Ridge on continuous target_rank_21.

No parameter sweep in Phase A.

## Primary stability comparison
For each test year, train natively on Repeat1 and Repeat3, predict on identical Repeat2 test features.
Report:
- mean absolute percentile-rank difference
- mean daily Spearman between Repeat1-trained and Repeat3-trained predictions
- Top1 disagreement fraction
- Top5 set Jaccard

Aggregate by simple mean across the four frozen years.

## Predictive quality
Train on Repeat2 and predict Repeat2.
Report:
- daily Spearman(prediction, target_rank_21)
- NDCG@5
- NDCG@10
- mean realized target percentile of predicted Top1
- Top5 overlap with realized Top5

Quality is diagnostic only on this burned development sample.

## Efficiency and determinism
Report fit+predict wall time per year and repeat same-data fit twice for determinism.

## Promotion rule
A candidate is eligible for full 2017-2026 replay only if:
- rank_mean_abs improves >=25% vs XGB_CANONICAL, AND
- Top1 disagreement does not worsen, AND
- mean daily Spearman stability improves, AND
- predictive NDCG@5 >=95% of XGB_CANONICAL, AND
- no maturity/lookahead violation.

Efficiency breaks ties; CAGR is not used in Phase A promotion.

## Laya
Laya is not included in Phase A because it is a typed choice/score engine over text/JSON rather than a native grouped numeric learning-to-rank estimator, and published material warns that long option lists degrade accuracy. It remains an explicit Phase C fallback only if conventional LTR candidates fail stability/quality gates.
