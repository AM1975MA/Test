## Limits and next unclosed certification check

This audit **classifies missingness by dependencies, NOT raw-root cause**. `base_missing_unclassified_source_vs_lookback` includes legitimate listing-age warmup, intrinsically undefined moments/ranks and potentially source holes; the audit does not prove which one without opening the original frozen raw history and mapping the exact lookback and feature dependency. This is explicitly *not* a source quality PASS. Similarly, equality of masks between the three frozen vintages is not price accuracy or causal feature validity.
