# R0-N — native XGBoost pair construction: exhaustive default vs fixed topk=5 (preregistration)
**Frozen before any R0-N training · 9 October 2026 · Test research branch only.**

**Motivation from R0-M audit:** masking 20bp pairs in a Python custom loss improved one constructed Y-only perturbation but degraded tail selection in one of two toy worlds: **FAIL_JOINT_GATE**. That custom loss replaced the original Hessian and normalization, so it is NOT a faithful direct change to production `rank:pairwise`.

**New exact observed implementation detail:** On XGBoost **3.1.3**, unmodified `rank:pairwise` with canonical params serializes to `learner.objective.lambdarank_param.lambdarank_pair_method=topk` with `lambdarank_num_pair_per_sample=4294967295` (effectively **all** comparably labeled pairs for a small ETF query). The `ndcg@3` *metric* separately reports 3: do not conflate evaluation metric and training objective. Independent toy 4 groups ×18 rows at 12 rounds gave identical numeric scores for default vs explicit k=18 (maxabs 0), different scores at k=3. XGBoost documented `lambdarank_num_pair_per_sample` selects candidate top k for pair construction. Docs: https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/release_3.1.0/parameter.html.

**Single frozen alternative before running models:** Same built-in `rank:pairwise`, **no custom Python loss**, only `lambdarank_num_pair_per_sample=5` versus objective native **default** exhaustive. Both `lambdarank_pair_method=topk`. `k=5` relates to original diagnostic realized Top5 and avoids hard ETF elimination; **it is not optimized** to previous Yahoo CAGR. No k=3/10/20 sweep.

**Artificial tests only:** fresh independent RNG worlds seeds `20261109` and `20261110`, generated with fixed `generate_world` function from source R0-M: each 32×32 grouped 125-feature training observations and independent 20×32 evaluated synthetic examples with nonlinear latent returns. Exactly one inner-query rank-order inversion 0.1bp by deterministic construction; independent training-X-only jitter σ2.5e-6; original training otherwise identical and evaluation X fixed. X-only training Y identical; Y-only training X identical. Three canonical seeds `101,202,303`, **360 rounds**, depth4, eta .035, hist, min_child_weight8, L2 8, L1 .1, subsample .85, colsample .8, original and modified models each. **2 worlds ×2 pair policies ×3 scenarios (base, X-only, Y-only) ×3 seeds =36 native XGB fits**, no original 149 ETF train or full backtest.

**Outcomes and decision predeclared:** for each world and arm, average the three seed raw predictions, then compute baseline synthetic chosen Top1 membership of latent future Top5, exact best, mean selected latent forward return and NDCG@5. Compare Top1 agreement after X-only and Y-only training, mean cross-ETF rank-MAD. A candidate advances only if **(i)** no decline in Top5 capture *in either world*, **(ii)** no degradation in synthetic selected mean realized return *in either world*, **(iii)** no decline in X-only or Y-only Top1 agreement *in either world*, **and** (iv) improved robustness in at least one dimension without collapse of full ranking. If any criterion fails -> `FAIL_JOINT_GATE`; **no tuning k after results**. Passing is `SYNTHETIC_ONLY_PROMISING`, never permission to modify V2 or claim CAGR.

**Data audit:** strict group row counts, label reversal exactly one real pair, count 36 successful 360-round fits, capture per-seed/ensemble outputs if possible, log source, model configs/hash and result JSON, pytest RED→GREEN for analytical helpers. No reuse of R0-F/H or R0-M artificial seeds. This design is a targeted falsifier of topk as a robustness control, not a random search.
