# R0-K preregistration: single native-XGBoost ranking-objective challenger, artificial data only
**Frozen before any R0-K training, 2026-10-09.** Research `AM1975MA/Test` branch `research/v2-xgb-immutable-checkpoints-20261008`.

**Evidence motivation:** R0-J matched frozen Yahoo Repeat1/3 price-derived Compact21 relevance labels and found exactly eight strict rank-pair inversions (plus 7 tie-created/7 tie-broken transitions) in 267 monthly comparisons. All eight strictly inverted pairs have **maximum absolute future return gap ≤0.0116 bp**, and all are **below the top decile** of training relevance. These facts are descriptive only; a label-only fix does not address proven independent training-X instability.

**One candidate, not a grid:** original `rank:pairwise` vs native `rank:ndcg` with `ndcg_exp_gain=false` to support Compact21 0..100 labels. The latter weights pairwise ranking gradients by ranking impact (per XGBoost 3.1.3 documentation); it is a technically motivated *hypothesis*, not an established fix. Use original XGBoost 3.1.3 and parameters unchanged: depth4, eta .035, minchild8 Hessian, lambda8, alpha .1, 360 boosting rounds per 101/202/303 seed, 125 features, one group per synthetic month; same default pair sampler in both, no extra topk tuning. A small real execution feasibility check with original 0..100 labels already passed on grouped synthetic data; no real ETF fit.

**Frozen test before results:** two independent synthetic worlds A/B, new seeds 20261023 / 20261024, 36 groups of 40 train and 12 groups of 40 independent evaluation. Three variants per world/objective/seed: unperturbed, X-only changed by 2.5e-6 Gaussian jitter, y-only *swap of two adjacent medium-relevance graded rows*, guaranteeing exactly one true ordinal pair inversion with two changed labels and retaining all top-most grades. **2 worlds × 2 objectives ×3 variants×3 seeds =36 360-round fits**. Same perturbation and eval inputs within each matched contrast. No Yahoo repeats/canonical Original149 training or backtest.

**Primary gates:** for each world independently baseline latent true-Top5 hit on 12 groups and NDCG@5; y-only Top1 agreement and X-only Top1 agreement with baseline. A stable model that loses top5 skill on either world is **not** good enough; no selection of best seed, world, fit, objective by CAGR. Report score/rank differences and if candidate does not jointly improve training perturbation tolerance and preserve rare positive-tail capture, mark **NO GO** on this diagnostic rather than tuning another objective parameter.

**Read-only source security:** R0-J QA on original price ZIP, exact ticker alignment, pair dates/margins, and previous R0-G count reconciliation; R0-K uses no ETF data whatsoever. Neither run changes `Etf_trader` or production source. No promotion, no financial CAGR claim.
