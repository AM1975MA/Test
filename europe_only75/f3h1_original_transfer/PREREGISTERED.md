H2 score = (q10+2q50+q90)/4, H3=H1+H2. Those future factor outcomes ONLY create training labels matured strictly before next signal, never current decision information.

At least 40 matured monthly training anchors, evaluate ~64 dates, strict one-Open embargo. Quarterly refit F3 and H models, do not touch parameters to optimize Euro outcomes.
