# T149-J — Predict F3 minus H1 basket economic value; frozen before first run
19 October 2026. Original **149 ETFs**, 64 already-repeatedly-mined 2021–26 trading blocks. This does NOT create independent OOS data. Repository Test ONLY; no changes to etf_trader.

## Baseline/falsifiable problem
F3 vs H1 average 0.52/5 shared ETFs, monthly log return correlation 0.590; static real-share 50/50 historical CAGR 23.22%, MaxDD −24.84%, Sharpe 0.887, daily ES5 −3.30%. A posthoc USO>QQQ 63d factor switch reported 29.38% CAGR with worse MaxDD −29.22%, signed monthly uplift CI crossing zero and failure in 2024. Previous rolling expert winners and 8-market-feature online ridge failed. There is NO proven regime-timing skill.

Instead predict portfolio-level **future 21-day cash-funded F3−H1 log return differential** conditioned on actual CURRENT expert basket composition and as-of macro/risk information. Prediction labels come ONLY from independently costed F3 and H1 archived hypothetical portfolios at the NEXT OPEN, available only after their full 21-session outcome maturity; **at signal for eval block k, last usable label is k−2 (never k−1)**. The current portfolio and all scorer features are frozen before outcome. Label is an explanatory approximation for basket-difference payoff; portfolio allocator is evaluated separately at exact shares and 10bp/side, not blended wealth paths.

## Strict previous-close as-of meta-features (10 features)
Current archived experts' frozen Top5 F3/H1, original frozen F3/H1/H3 per-ticker future prediction scores, all 149 as-of Close prices, prior-close market history, current frozen factor betas:
- `F3_holding_relative_score_advantage` = (mean frozen F3 score on F3 picks minus mean frozen F3 score on H1 picks)/std frozen F3 score across 149; positive by selection.
- `H1_holding_relative_score_advantage` = (mean frozen H1 score on H1 picks minus mean frozen H1 score on F3 picks)/std frozen H1 score across 149; positive.
- `score_advantage_difference` = first - second, fixed unitless ex ante confidence comparison (NOT calibrated across models).
- `F3_H1_basket_past63_momentum_diff` = mean equally-weighted previous 63 *Close-to-Close* log return on five current F3 names minus H1 names.
- `F3_H1_basket_past63_vol_ratio` = log 63d realized std of frozen five-ETF equally-weighted daily log returns F3 / H1.
- `F3_H1_past63_basket_corr` = historical as-of 63d daily correlation between two equal-weighted current selected baskets.
- `F3_H1_quoted_factor_beta_distance` = Euclidean distance of 10 as-of beta exposure *mean* vectors for F3 and H1 baskets, conservative explanatory diagnostic (not direct direction).
- `F3_H1_residual_quantile_width_difference` = mean current R_Q90−R_Q10 on F3 holdings minus H1 holdings (already-fitted as-of frozen H3 residual forecaster, *not future observed residual*).
- `USO_minus_QQQ_past63` = previous 63 completed Close return USO−QQQ, posthoc known explanation from previous experiment, included ONLY as predeclared feature with explicit prior-data contamination.
- `SPY_vol21_over_vol63` = as-of market volatility ratio indicating turbulence.
All 10 must be finite from as-of data, no return at trade Open or later. StandardScaler and all meta-train fit exclusively previous **fully matured** relative portfolio labels; never use noncausal hindsight oracle strategy from previous experiments.

## Three new policies, fixed hyperparams without sweeping
- **J1_SCORE_DIFFERENCE_RULE** completely untrained. If score_advantage_difference > +0.5 allocate 75% F3 /25% H1; if below −0.5 allocate 25% F3/75% H1; else keep 50/50. This is an arbitrary predeclared unitless rank margin — not a calibrated expected-return estimate.
- **J2_ONLINE_RIDGE_PROB**: after at least 18 fully matured monthly samples, train StandardScaler + Ridge(alpha=25, fit_intercept=True) on all 10 as-of portfolio features to predict the **F3−H1 next21 log payoff**. Historical residual std on TRAIN only, floor 0.03 monthly, calibrated Normal z= predicted payoff/sigma (no claim of Gaussian correctness). Allocate 75/25 only if posterior z exceeds ±0.50; otherwise static50. Before 18 train samples always static50; recorded probability of F3 advantage = Phi(z). Run one monthly fit per anchor with strict embargo; no cross-sample lookahead.
- **J3_ONLINE_ANALOG_SIGN**: after at least 18 matured samples, using scaler fitted on past only, find **eight nearest** historical as-of observations by Euclidean distance on **only 5 features**: score_advantage_difference, past63 basket momentum diff, basket vol ratio, USO−QQQ past63, SPY vol21/63. Fit nothing other than historical scaler; predict signed monthly mean of nearest eight realized F3−H1 returns and train-only neighbor sd with floor 0.03. If z>+0.5 allocate 75% F3/25% H1; z<−0.5 allocate 25/75, otherwise 50/50. Always only matured dates. No causal interpretation of nearest-neighbor by itself.
Compare with F3, H1, H3, frozen historical STATIC_F3_H1_50, frozen OIL_VS_GROWTH_63 and prior weak ONLINE_RIDGE_GATE, all reconstructed exact 1,345 daily capital points with <1e−9 errors. Rebalance actual underlying ETF shares monthly at next Open and 10bp per side, no leverage.

## Primary scientific and economic stopping rules
1. **Prediction**: J2/J3 squared error predicting independent F3−H1 monthly net log differential must improve on rolling matured historical mean baseline, AND sign Brier improve on rolling matured historical P(F3>H1) prior; report directional accuracy 64 periods, first 18 forced-neutral months not considered as model test; logloss proper-scored with clipped probabilities, 3-anchor bootstrap incremental MSE with lower bound of improvement >0 (bootstrap anchors, not ETF rows). Metric may fail.
2. **Allocation**: paired monthly net growth J1/J2/J3 minus static50 must have 3-anchor circular-block 95% CI lower bound >0, historical CAGR >static50, MaxDD >= static50 (less negative), daily ES5 >= static50 (less severe) and improve in BOTH 2021–23 and 2024–26. Else scientific FAIL and do not select best historical CAGR by posterior shopping.
3. **Controls/causal**: next-open capital curve parity vs archived F3/H1/H3, static50 and old oil gate; mutate prices AFTER signal and future expert label k−1 onward, assert zero impact on current meta forecast; same 64 anchor dates, original 149 source SHA; every allocation weight is 0.25,0.5,0.75 F3 and remaining H1.
4. **Calibration**: record per-month absolute residual, predicted signed differential, fitted historical sigma, estimated positive-outperformance probability, and all 149 scenario rankings; diagnose positive vs negative market factor USO/QQQ, 2022 and 2024 failures but do NOT change model after reading evaluation outcomes.
5. **NO new independent information**: signals derived solely from old retrospectively adjusted prices and pretrained F3/H1/H3 original 149 forecasting artifacts; known dataset repeated evaluation/multiple comparisons means no validated prospective alpha regardless of in-sample result; initial warmup scores are not genuine independent prospective calls. User target >30% CAGR is aspirational, never a threshold for selecting a past-winner.
