# T149-K — Prequential walk-forward machine learning STACKING of frozen F3 and H1
Frozen 19 Oct 2026, before first execution. Original **149 ETFs** and exactly **64 archived 21-trading-session anchors 2021–2026**. This is a heavily reused survivor-selected USD US ETF sample, NOT an untouched or EU broker-executable dataset; no 30% CAGR promise. Do not modify etf_trader.

## Key difference from failed T149-J relative monthly ML
T149-J trained an online Ridge and an 8-analog model with only ~45 month-level labels (outcome F3−H1 portfolio differential); neither beat an online historical prior Brier/MSE. T149-K learns cross-sectional interaction of *frozen genuinely prequential F3 and H1 ticker-by-ticker scores* with **149 observations per monthly anchor**, conditional rank and risk, but conservatively recognizes within-month dependence: 149 tickers on one date are NOT 149 independent macro scenarios.

Scores `B0_F3_FROZEN` and `H1_FACTOR_ONLY` from `research_reset/predict149/factor_residual/results/FULL_9536_ASOF_FACTOR_AND_RESIDUAL_FORECASTS.csv`, with `v63`, `R_Q10`, `R_Q90`, categorical `category`, `future_excess`, `future_log`, and `maturity`. Original frozen predictions were fitted chronologically on earlier matured labels, making these permissible prior model outputs for a next-stage prequential learner. No training from future outcomes of current or incomplete previous month.

## Fixed 12-dimensional feature vector per ETF, per decision
As-of cross-sectional ranks of F3 and H1 (0..1), their mean, their difference, ETF 63-day volatility cross-sectional rank, as-of frozen quantile spread `R_Q90−R_Q10` cross-sectional rank; six one-hot original macro categories. No prospective realized factor or returns. Dynamic features use only scores predicted **at signal Close** and preceding 63d vol. Dates with different ticker rosters FAIL. Target shifted 21-trading-day following first Open to subsequent Open, 60%SPY/40%AGG benchmark relative log excess (same archived). Technical constraint: for eval index k the newest truly fully matured label is **k−2**, because k−1 does not mature until the current trade Open AFTER decision previous Close; guard by `maturity_open < signal`. Use no 'retrospective full-sample scaler'. Models refit every 3 evaluation months, ONLY prior matured meta rows; begin after 12 matured anchors, so initial 13 eval months use unchanged static F3/H1 50:50. For an evaluation anchor the stored prediction must be genuinely from its own past model, not a base model refit with all historic outcomes.

## Experimental arms, NO hyperparameter selection
- B0 true cash-funded frozen `STATIC_F3_H1_50`, plus F3, H1, H3 original frozen backtest strict machine precision parity across all 1345 daily Open points.
- B1 `EQUAL_RANK_TOP5`: simple static sum of within-month F3 and H1 percentiles and buy 5 maximum; no ML, to distinguish score pooling from ML.
- K1 `ML_SIGNED_TAIL_HEADS`: two L2-regularized logistic classification heads `P(future ETF signed benchmark-excess > +.03)`, `P(future signed excess < -.05)` with `LogisticRegression(C=.05,max_iter=250,solver="liblinear",random_state=20261019)`. Rank all 149 by **0.03*P(up)-0.05*P(down)**; select 5 equal-20%-weight ETF positions, 10bps per buy/sale. Record calibrated proper Brier/log-loss against the fully matured online event prior. Evaluate on 51 post-warmup month anchors.
- K2 `ML_SIGNED_MEAN_GBDT`: `HistGradientBoostingRegressor(loss="squared_error",max_iter=70,max_leaf_nodes=5,max_depth=2,min_samples_leaf=100,l2_regularization=30,learning_rate=.03,random_state=20261019)`, fitting **clipped** signed ETF future benchmark relative excess ±20% and ranking its expected clipped signed payoff. Compare signed clipped MSE against online fully matured unconditional prior and against **simple non-ML frozen H1/F3**.
- K3 `ML_GRADED_LAMBDARANK`: `LGBMRanker(objective="lambdarank",n_estimators=70,num_leaves=7,max_depth=3,min_child_samples=100,reg_lambda=25,learning_rate=.04,label_gain=[0,1,3,7],random_state=20261019,n_jobs=1,verbosity=-1)`, each completed past anchor a **149-name query** labelled future ranking top5=3, next10=2, next15=1 else=0. Graded ranker may favor volatility; test realized signed mean and bottom15.
- K4 `ML_RELATIVE_BASKET_GATE`: use same K2 signed monthly per-ETF predictions, compute average predicted ETF signed excess of frozen F3 holdings minus frozen H1 holdings at each signal; **if >+0.02 choose F3 75%/H1 25%; if <−.02 F3 25%/H1 75%; else unchanged 50:50**. Never use realized future return at decision. Actual merged ETFs with 10bps share-level cost as old engine, not weighted sum of NAVs.

## Outcome and falsification
Causality tests: for EVERY predicted month all fitted samples satisfy label_maturity OPEN < decision close, no current or prior unfinished labels; modifying all FUTURE labels after k−2 MUST NOT change model prediction at k. Exact 149×64 signals from previously frozen F3 and H1, no future market features. Historical base model predictions prequential by record. No new model imported/trained with test data.

Primary gate for each learnable K1-4 requires:
- same 64-anchor paired net log growth vs static F3/H1 50:50 block-3 bootstrap 95% **lower CI >0**, cash-funded CAGR higher, MaxDD at least as good (less negative), worst daily5% ES at least as good, and positive incremental signed payoff vs 50:50 in BOTH 2021–23 and 2024–26;
- K1 proper Brier score both event probabilities better than all-history matured online event priors, K2's clipped return MSE below matured prior, K3 realized future top5 signed excess beats simple equal rank top5 in both eras, K4 K2 MSE and full economic gates. ANY missing = FAIL, even if CAGR 30%+.
Diagnostic precision@10 for future signed top15, bottom15 of selected 5, their forward signed benchmark relative profit, mean within-month future-excess Spearman, NDCG@10. Proper probability scoring at eval dates after warmup ONLY to avoid as-if fitted information. Paired resampling groups 64 **dates** not tickers. Secondary per-era risk and turnover and simple score pooling and relevant 60/40 passive benchmark.
