# T149-P1 — Best-ticker predictive skill tournament — FROZEN BEFORE FIRST RUN
2026-10-19, isolated research branch `research/149-predictive-ticker-tournament-20261019`.

## Scope and scientific target
The user rightly prioritizes *the best possible forecaster of future high-performing ETF tickers*, ahead of any optimizer or switching policy. 30 percentage points in CAGR is an **aspiration, not a mathematically guaranteed outcome**. This study asks which predictor, if any, can identify and economically exploit future winning ETF names *before their return occurs*, net of 10bp actual one-way switching costs, on EXACTLY the historical 149 ETFs from `evidence_v1/data/original149` (SHA `7bccf1b756bf3652280a254c7486fb43f1e805ebb4cdf10731cecc74007292e6`). Source fund list is biased by 2026 survival and its 2017–26 returns have been mined in earlier experiments; this is developmental, **not an untouched holdout nor EUR broker-executable data**.

Freeze the cross-sectional observation unit: every 21 trading sessions, from the study calendar at `2017-02-01` until the last complete block ending `2026-06-11`, exactly 112 complete monthly-like blocks. At each block beginning t, **information includes only data through prior completed Close s < t OPEN**, no future Open, no observation after s. Future outcome of candidate i: `log(Open_i[t+21]/Open_i[t])`. For objective economic comparability, define `excess_i = future_log_i - log(.60*future_SPY_ratio+.40*future_AGG_ratio)`; this is **not** simply raw return. Evaluate also `top_decile_i = 1` if ticker i is among **15 highest absolute next-21-day log-return ETFs** in that same 149. This future label is used ONLY after maturity.

## Fixed unlike-each-other forecasters
All five make a score or estimate for each of 149 before the target 21-session return matures. No copying OASIS ranking or prior ML model weights.
- **N0_MOMENTUM63**: past63 log-return, simple nonparametric past leadership baseline.
- **N1_REVERSAL5**: negative past5 log-return, structural short-term mean-reversion baseline.
- **N2_RIDGE**: pooled 149-ETF (not winner-only) regression of next21 log EXCESS return, fitted with `StandardScaler` on historical matured samples and `Ridge(alpha=100)`; group one-hot; 19 as-of price, factor, realized-risk, recent acceleration and beta features.
- **N3_HGB_RETURN**: nonlinear `HistGradientBoostingRegressor(max_iter=65,max_leaf_nodes=7,min_samples_leaf=80,learning_rate=.05,l2_regularization=20,early_stopping=False,random_state=20261019)`, same 19 features, pooled future excess targets; training data ONLY anchors whose target exit occurred before signal. Fit **quarterly** at every third 21d evaluation anchor, reuse old trained model for intermediate months (therefore no future leakage).
- **N4_HGB_TOPDECILE**: equally constrained nonlinear `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` with exact same complexity parameters, target top15 future absolute-return names per anchor. Predict `P(top15)` scores (including truthful proper Brier/logloss); fit at same quarterly cadence.
- **N5_HIERARCHICAL_RESIDUAL**: `Ridge(alpha=100)` on as-of difference to macro-category factor for each ticker rather than its total return, with per-category intercept and asset residual feature; predicted ETF excess = predicted own factor historical momentum proxy + estimated idiosyncratic excess, all from s, never future. This isolates economic mechanism distinct from generic return fitting.

Group factor labels are explicitly crude original six classes; real assets are economically heterogeneous. Do not pretend a statistical factor is a valid commodity-roll or Treasury risk model.

## Primary scientific decision metrics
Evaluate 2021–26 on identical anchors:
1. **Mean Spearman** cross-sectional per anchor between `predicted_score_i` and `forward21 excess_i`, with 95% 3-anchor circular-block CI.
2. **NDCG@10** from sorting predicted scores versus nonnegative realized excess gains (not raw max winner's leverage); plus mean forward excess log return of predicted top1/top3/top5 and Precision@10 for highest **15** realized absolute-return ETFs. The formal rate of top-decile under uniform random is 15/149, but volatility-and-group-matched controls also needed for claims.
3. **Top5 portfolio**: 5 equal dollar initial weights at each 21-session Open, self-financing exact 10bp each side, buy and hold shares until next 21-session rebalance (no intramonth leaking). For each model: annualized 2021–26 net CAGR, daily Open MaxDD, ES5, gross factor/categorical concentration, turnover, positive months. Compare SPY and 60/40 SPY/AGG buy-hold on same period with their own exact 10bp buy fills, flag tax/FX/spread missing. This is a shadow economic probe only, models evaluated many times on heavily reused data. NEVER choose a "winner" merely from a high CAGR.
4. **Calibration N4**: top15 event Brier and logloss versus online historical 15/149 constant-base probability, by era 2021–23 versus 2024–26; variable confidence for rare alpha is meaningless if uncalibrated.
5. **Nonstationary skill**: first 2021–23 and second 2024–26, plus 3-anchor bootstrap vs N0 and group/category+volatility null for any promising candidate.

## Gate; no 30% via data-mining
A true research candidate must show (a) positive 95% lower CI of mean Spearman on chronological 21-session anchors; (b) group-conditioned ranking improvement not simply variance-tail extreme; (c) net CAGR > benchmark 60/40 AND daily DD no more severe than −30% (net sharpe and ES5 reported); (d) both eras positive economic edge vs passive. >30% annual CAGR with all gates would be interesting **only exploratory evidence**, not proof of deployment. No hyperparameter sweeps, no leveraged portfolios, no options, no simulated foresight. None of these alternative models is assumed to succeed.
