# M0 — physics of economic opportunities: FROZEN protocol
2026-10-19, isolated branch `research/149-opportunity-physics-m0-20261019`.

This is NOT OASIS V3. It is a first-principles hypothesis test of whether economically interesting ETF rotations persist enough to be identified without hindsight. The 149 original US ETF raw Open/Close CSVs, manifest SHA and six coarse research labels are frozen; retrospective survivor list is NOT a PIT investment universe or EU broker-tradable list. Evaluate 2017-02-01–2026-07-01 with first 112 nonoverlapping full 21-trading-session blocks (2366 observed study opens); signal prior close (strictly earlier than entry Open), trade at entry Open. Exclude final incomplete block from performance; report dates and discarded observations. Original `etf_trader` remains untouched.

### Seven non-negotiable lenses
1. **Perfect foresight baseline**: in each 21-day block, select the single ETF with highest *future* Open-to-Open log return among all 149; deduct 10bp per trade side, consistent transition accounting. This ORACLE is non-causal and cannot be monetized. Use it only as a diagnostic upper-quality bound, NOT mathematically exact solution to globally constrained optimum because choosing monthly gross winner ignores interblock trading costs.
2. **Recognition-delay diagnostic**: freeze exactly the same hindsight-selected winners but force their execution after d=0,1,3,5,10 trading sessions. Existing position held until delayed switch, first starts with SPY; same cost. A delayed clairvoyant is **not** a feasible trading strategy and can outperform zero delay occasionally. Report gross value change, concentration of advantage and sign. Do not optimize d.
3. **True causal controls**: prices visible at prior close only, choose strongest prior 21-day momentum and 63-day momentum; simple 63d Top5 sticky hysteresis retains incumbent if still in Top5; broad six-class peer-filter strategy chooses class with strongest past63 group mean, then strongest individual 63d within that class. Always 100% one ETF, no ex-post future feature. Fee 10bp each buy/sell; cash at zero where applicable.
4. **Matched-exposure placebo**: 250 deterministic random portfolios constrained to follow exactly the class and switch/no-switch decision schedule of past63 momentum, drawn without reference to future return. Compare returns with random distribution, not to a posterior predictive p-value. If causal momentum fails these controls, no new learned strategy is justified.
5. **Do opportunities persist?** Metrics of past Top5 vs future 21-day best success, past vs future rank Spearman per 21d anchor, relative net payoff of past winner vs future oracle, opportunity gross margin and net 20bp cost. Also evaluate precommitted 1/3/5/10/21/42/63 forward returns and relative SPY/AGG reference of the prior63-selected instrument, using past Close as-of only; note longer forward windows overlap time anchors.
6. **Events**: diagnostic *ex-post* distinction: severe SPY block loss <=-6%, rebound if previous SPY21 <=-6% and current SPY block gain >=+6%; else ordinary. Do NOT pass event flags to causal selectors (future leakage). Report oracle and causal benchmark differences, sample sizes.
7. **Honest comparators**: passive buy-hold SPY and static 60% SPY/40% AGG initial allocations with same adjusted USA Open and nominal initial 10bp, **not identical EUR cash, bid-ask, withholding or ETF access**. Report CAGR, terminal wealth, 21-session sampled drawdown (NOT daily DD), switches, fees/turnover. Historical already mined, no statistical clean holdout.

**Predeclared diagnostic interpretation:**
- HUGE oracle CAGR says only that hindsight volatility lets a clairvoyant win. It **DOES NOT** prove learnability. The decisive discriminant is whether *causal* prior21/63/sticky/class-filter controls beat same-cost/time/exposure random placebo and simple passive in both returns and tail risk; whether previous top5 reliably predicts winners and whether opportunity remains after d=5 or 10.
- If opportunity selection is almost random or forecastability vanishes while oracle retains absurd advantage, **NO GO** to adaptive rotation from OHLCV. Need new causal information or different assets/horizon. If skill exists but weak, follow with independent estimation of attainable skill and proper cross-validation under true new vintages. No switching model or stochastic optimizer may be built until that gate.
- This research is preregistered for the original149; all hindsight and cross-section winner computations MUST be labeled non-causal in JSON outputs.
- Save per-block winners, policy ETF picks, realized returns, category attribution, 250 null terminal outcomes and scientific verdict with hashes. Validate leakage with future-quote perturbation unit test.

**Sharp limitation:** a 21-session block ORACLE does not replicate a constrained globally optimal stochastic-control policy; and simply introducing a delay into a signal with perfect future knowledge does not measure an implementable recognition latency. This experiment is necessary to map opportunity amplitude and distinguish "lots of hindsight" from even weakly informative causal signals; it is not sufficient to prove any tradable edge.
