## Forensic update — Compact21 attribution and quantization

Confirmed findings:
- Execution ablation: freezing repeat2 decisions collapses cross-raw CAGR span from 4.9236 pp to ~0.000022 pp. Downstream execution/risk/stop/V6 is not a material source of the dispersion.
- MA3 corrected targets are highly stable across raw repeats (~0.19–0.20% changed cells, mean absolute difference ~5.7e-6).
- Titanium component ablation identifies Compact21 as the dominant amplifier. Compact21 rank changes in ~92–93% of cells with mean absolute rank difference ~0.053–0.054; Tail rank changes in only ~5.8–6.5% with mean absolute difference ~0.00041–0.00047; macro boost is identical.
- Compact21 XGB attribution shows inference-feature perturbations alone are nearly irrelevant (mean rank difference ~0.000105, daily Spearman ~0.999992, Top1 disagreement 0%). The instability is generated by training sensitivity. Training-feature-only and training-label-only perturbations both materially change the fitted XGBRanker.
- Operational lookahead static audit is clean. Byte-preserved future mutation/truncation makes all inspected features, matured targets, Titanium and XGB_TAIL exactly invariant. A 24-row ET_TAIL residual remains under investigation; direct same-rowset ET refit produced exact equality, so no evidence currently attributes those rows to future information.
- Feature quantization diagnostic: Q4 (4 decimals on all 125 Compact21 F2D inputs) raises cross-snapshot exact feature equality to ~92% in training and reduces Compact21 rank mean difference by ~18%. Q3 is rejected diagnostically because it does not improve rank stability. Q4 full-pipeline test is preregistered and in progress.
