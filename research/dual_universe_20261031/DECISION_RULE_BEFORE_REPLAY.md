# Operational definitions fixed before portfolio outputs

This addendum resolves implementation details of PROTOCOL.md before any new portfolio scorecard is evaluated. It does not change model hyperparameters after training outcomes.

Common comparator: BASE_H, Top2, equal weights, monthly rebalance without rank buffer, half exposure below the prior-signal local market MA200, 5.5% stop, 10bp one-way fees. This is DUAL-01 control, not an unchanged legacy V2/Stage19. The candidate set includes unmodified RETURN_H Top2 equal-weight as an RH-50-inspired comparator, but its causal engine and corrected common features make it a new version.

The portfolio window begins at the first common supported monthly signal: EU native Hybrid requires two actual prior months and is unavailable in January/February 2019. Use the first next-session entry common to both (expected April 2019), not cash-padded January 2019 performance. Preserve later missing monthly signals as carry-forward holdings for all candidates, and record coverage. Require at least 20 contemporaneously eligible ETFs per market and common months; never use future label availability for live eligibility.

Weights: inverse volatility is based on signal-date 63-session volatility, capped at 60% with proportional redistribution. Margin allocation on Top2 is w1=.6+.4*clip((score1-score2)/.12,0,1), w2=1-w1; all score inputs are standardized rank scales. This is a declared legacy-inspired policy, not an unchanged old allocator. Regime exposure is set only on scheduled monthly events from the signal-close market state. Rank buffer retains held names in Top(2K), then fills slots by the signal ranking. Missing executable Open for an existing holding delays the entire scheduled rebalance; a missing new-buy Open leaves that slot's weight in cash. No forward/backfilled trade prices. Stop reference is the average cost for existing positions with additions; reductions keep the anchor. A stop at a pre-established level fills at worse Open for a gap and at the level for an intraday touch, less 10bp slippage; fees remain additional. No same-session re-entry of a stopped name. Cash earns zero.

All 15 selectors and all 40 permissible allocation/risk combinations (600 policies) run in each universe at 10bp and 30bp: 2,400 paths, not 2,400 independent observations. Recomputing baseline costs at each level preserves paired comparisons. Include no-stop, full-exposure, momentum-only, residual and Ridge alternatives rather than reject them from earlier significance verdicts.

For a concrete research specification, rank candidates by the WORSE of the USA and EU mean annualized log-return improvements over the shared comparator in the development window ending 2024, using 30bp stress costs. Require non-worsening observed development MaxDD in each market relative to the same-cost comparator. Report the complete risk/return frontier and uncertainty, not merely a winner. This conservative drawdown constraint is an analyst-selected research rule, not the user's personal risk limit or a proof of future safety. No confidence-interval-zero or posterior-95% threshold is used. Tie within numerical tolerance: fewer active mechanisms then deterministic configuration order. If no challenger qualifies, retain the comparator and report why.

This ranking selects a candidate only for research; historical multiple-testing bias remains. Evaluate 2021–2024 sensitivity and 2025/2026 separately without selecting or retuning from those later results. A dynamically selected annual specification is optional and requires an actual single-account replay; splicing independent NAVs is not an executable strategy. Every proposed incremental mechanism must retain paired USA/EU comparisons, including adverse cases. Previously missing raw-data/model identities remain blockers; uncertainty including zero is not a blocker by itself.
