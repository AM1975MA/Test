# One causal research router, fixed before economic scorecards

This specifies the optional chronological comparison in PROTOCOL.md before any new portfolio outcomes are inspected. It is not a new optimization after seeing DUAL-01 winners.

The fixed 840-policy comparison can only identify a historically favorable specification. Add one predeclared causal policy-selection algorithm as a separate candidate, DUAL-SELECT:

- Prior to at least 24 complete common monthly outcomes, use the declared DUAL-01 comparator (BASE_H K2 EQ monthly, regime half-exposure and stop ON, no rank buffer).
- At each January 1 from 2022 onward, select a single specification for BOTH markets using only outcomes ending December 31 of the prior year. Use the same 30bp cost-stress worst-market log-return improvement rule and no-worse observed MaxDD constraint relative to the same comparator, over the entire available prior history. Do not use current-year returns, labels or future annual decisions.
- No posterior-probability cutoff. Ties: fewer active controls, then configuration identifier. If no beneficial eligible challenger, retain the comparator. No local US winner and separate EU winner.
- Apply the elected rule from that calendar year in actual single-account self-financing replays in both markets, with holdings, cash, stop anchors and costs carried across changes. Do not splice equity curves from the independently simulated candidates. Score forecasts for an event remain those available at its own signal date.
- Report DUAL-SELECT against the fixed comparator and the retrospectively selected development candidate, separately before 2025 and in 2025/2026. A failed router is retained in evidence, not retuned. Annual elections and their maximum data dates are persisted and tested for prefix invariance.

This protects the implemented annual decisions from use of later outcomes, but it does not make this extensively explored historical research a pristine prospective validation or eliminate universe survival bias. It adds exactly one selector algorithm, not permission to optimize router window length or thresholds.
