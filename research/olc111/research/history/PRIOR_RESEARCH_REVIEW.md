# OLC111 — Evidence review before the next experiment

**16 November 2026 · AM1975MA/Test · research only**

## Decision supported by the history

The repository does **not** support another moving-average stop, a new keep-buffer, an automatic return-to-the-old-ETF rule, or another small economic Ridge justified only by having more ETF rows. These families have already been tested repeatedly. The most defensible immediate extension is a **daily, execution-aligned comparison of cash, actual holdings and a frozen candidate set**, with a small genuinely incremental information block and explicit controls for the information already tried.

The options **term structure** is a distinguishable next information hypothesis. OLC34 used only VIX level and its 21-session log change for monthly relative substitution events. It did **not** test VIX9D/VIX or VIX/VIX3M for daily absolute cash-versus-risk decisions. This distinction should be documented, and the old VIX block should remain a control.

This review retrieved **75 source documents/scripts across 45 research branches**, plus the recent frozen reports and selected earlier V3 reports. Exact repository paths, immutable commits, blob identities and original locations of copied reports are in [SOURCE_MANIFEST.json](SOURCE_MANIFEST.json). Local readable copies are in `sources/`. No financial simulation, remote write or workflow change was performed here. The review does not claim to have inspected every binary artifact or to have discovered results where only a protocol/downloader exists.

More importantly, **57.29%/59.38% of chosen positions never reached the top quintile at 21 sessions**, whereas only 9.38%/16.67% first became leaders and then lost leadership by 42 sessions. This suggests substantial initial-selection error; it does not imply that another residual learner can capture the oracle gap. ## 5. Two immediate experiments and one later qualification

### A. Daily economic action values, with cash as an explicit alternative

**Hypothesis:** information observable before the decision can distinguish harmful exposure from recoverable drawdown and identify when a candidate exceeds both holding and cash after costs.

Keep the OLC109 selection recipe and candidate eligibility frozen. Score a bounded action set from the **same actual account state**, including cash and pending orders. Use execution-aligned absolute and differential payoff labels, causal maturity/embargo and date-balanced training. Preserve price-only, train-only mean/base-rate, always-follow-selection and static-exposure controls.

**What is new:** daily cash-versus-invested decisions and the precise execution-aligned payoff question. **What is old:** HOLD/SWITCH, incumbent features, peer context, nonlinear learners and common-state branching. Daily sampling does not make overlapping outcomes independent.

**Falsification:** no incremental predictive or decision value over the train-only prior; no improvement over a static policy at comparable exposure/risk; benefit concentrated in a few overlapping events, one year or one exact fill timing; or empty decision changes. Report all outcomes even if the experiment fails. OLC02/04/70/74/99 give the relevant negative controls.

### B. Options term structure as an incremental input to the same action experiment

**Hypothesis:** the short-versus-medium implied-volatility shape provides information about near-term adverse moves/rebounds beyond the existing price state and VIX level/change.

Compare identical labels, learner, states and actions under **PRICE_ONLY**, **PRICE + legacy VIX level/change**, and **PRICE + legacy VIX + the small frozen term-structure block**. If using log(VIX9D/VIX) and log(VIX/VIX3M), specify handling of missing sessions, ratio alignment and the information timestamp before outcomes. A US same-calendar-day close cannot be assumed available at the earlier Xetra close; use the last genuinely published input.

**Falsification:** the term structure fails incremental forecast/decision loss against the legacy-VIX control; changes merely lower mean exposure without timing value; or apparent gains disappear with conservative publication lag, doubled costs, delayed fills or next-close execution. Broadening to VVIX/SKEW or searching many transforms after seeing results would create a new exploratory study, not confirmation of this one.

This hypothesis is materially different from OLC34, but still inherits its lesson: external data must beat a simple prior before their economic value can be claimed.

### C. Later: release-dated futures/positioning qualification

Before any portfolio rerun, determine whether a small **economically mapped** futures/positioning panel can be assembled with verified instrument identity, actual release timestamps, roll construction and enough non-overlapping history. Include a negative/control block to separate positional information from prices already present.

**Falsification/readiness failure:** report dates substituted for publication dates, index proxies presented as futures, unstable mappings to ETF exposures, insufficient coverage, or no incremental predictive value over price-only and options controls. If readiness fails, record that no predictive experiment was possible; do not invent an alpha verdict. OLC95/96 provide downloader starting points, not validated outcomes.

## 6. Methodological requirements that directly affect interpretation

1. **Count dates and distinct economic exposures.** 2,096 ETF rows, 45 pairs per month, cloned accounts, multiple seeds and overlapping daily labels are not independent regimes. Normalize sampling by date; avoid wrapper multiplicity deciding the learning weight.
2. **Measure the changed decision.** Separate forecast error, candidate selection, cash exposure and account P&L. AUC/recall, positive entry return versus cash, oracle gaps and a higher final CAGR answer different questions.
3. **Keep complete state in interventions.** The simulated effect is causal within fixed exogenous prices, not proof that a market action would cause the same real-world return.
4. **Audit genuine availability.** Current V3 catalog survivorship, adjustment vintages and the use of full-day positive volume in original Open validity remain limitations. US options timing adds another explicit as-of boundary.
5. **Use falsification controls without overstating validation.** Chronological fits and descriptive block intervals do not erase repeated use of 2019–2026. All prior failed families, changed hypotheses, adverse years and costs should remain visible. The next study can establish a better historical mechanism test; untouched future evidence is still required for a future-performance claim.
