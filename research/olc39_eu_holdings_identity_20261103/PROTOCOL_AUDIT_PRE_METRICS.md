## Deterministic audit, categories and evidence gates

1. Inventory every actual held EU primary ticker; average NAV share per calendar year and 2021–24, holding days and dates, source observed validity and weight sums. Reconcile sum all 27 to source primary budget and F2 8-ticker weights (OLC38).
2. Evidence catalog by exact **ticker + venue + ISIN + product name + issuer-tracked index + payout policy + documented effective date**. First, public issuer/exchange records as of 2026-11-03, then reliable third-party instrument pages. Current prospectus/index name is only `CURRENT_DESCRIPTION`, **not** verified historical 2021–2024 holdings or index construction. Mark `PIT_CONSTITUENTS_NOT_VERIFIED` unless a dated primary-source holdings/constituents snapshot for the decision date is actually found. If historical ISIN, index or share-class conflicts are found, mark `UNRESOLVED` and do not silently resolve by ticker guess. Never import 2026 sector weights to 2021. No construction of artificial historical ETF holdings.
3. Assign mutually exclusive **broad stated investment-mandate groups** (e.g., EU banks, EU tech, US tech, semiconductors, energy, travel/leisure, EU property, commodities/materials, fixed income duration). These are **proxy categories**, not security look-through; a listed-in-Europe fund can hold foreign equities and EUR trading denomination is not EUR FX hedging. Also aggregate a non-mutually-exclusive risk theme `technology_including_semis`, based on explicit mandates only; avoid double counting funds.
4. Produce year-by-year NAV exposure weights and time charts. Identify how much was directly in Travel & Leisure EXV9, whether it dominates years and whether its inclusion is a response to historical actual holdings, not a model recommendation. Flag dividend-distributing direct positions; no assumption that the archived Open is or isn't adjusted without raw vendor provenance.
5. **Prespecified descriptive travel-proxy ablation:** re-run OLC38 annual 252-observation nonnegative style clone (same SLSQP, regularization 1e-7, half-L1 legacy fee .001, 2021 clone 7 factors / 2022–24 8) on the EXACT 1021 common EU observed Open dates. Compare FULL 7/8-factor model with a single FIXED variant omitting EXV9 only (6 factors in 2021; 7 later). Reproduce OLC38 FULL NAV/returns first to tolerance 1e-9. Report common-day paired RMSE, correlation, annual errors, coefficient of EXV9 in original FULL, and the effect of omission; **do not** select a better-return clone, re-fit thresholds, claim alpha, or touch Meteorite. No other leave-one-out/research sweep.
6. Report PIT verification coverage separately at instrument-description level and historical-holdings level; no "all assets PIT mapped" conclusion from modern factsheets. Explicitly identify ticker/ISIN inconsistencies, predecessor index methodology/rebrands, ex-dividend and current-vs-historical data gaps. If any holdings as-of map is unverified, PIT quality gate stays FAIL.

## Expected source limitation

Modern iShares issuer pages give current product objective, ISIN and distribution; they usually do not certify constituent weights at archived 2021–2024 signal dates. Some issuer indexes were changed through time; the exact historical index must be evidenced by dated contemporaneous documents. A historical ticker can change identity/share class after issuer mergers. Count these as UNRESOLVED; do not use current classification to claim tested predictive economic exposures.
