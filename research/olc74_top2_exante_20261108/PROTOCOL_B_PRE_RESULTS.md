# OLC74B — supervised relative-return ranking from complete Top10 cross-pairs (post-OLC74A, pre-OLC74B)
Date 2026-11-08; this addendum was frozen after seeing OLC74A's negative prequential skill and FUSION_CONF USA in-sample opportunity. It is an exploratory follow-up, NOT independent OOS.

Research question: with just ~90 selected Top2 pairs per market, the OLC74A relative-return learner may be sample limited. Increase sample by **reusing all rank-top10 pairs already known at each date**, keeping the Top10 selected by original FUSION at the date. No new information, no alternate label target, no changes to OLC19 context/risk/timing.

Per market-month, sort the original FUSION candidates descending at signal_date. Form all unordered pairs in the 10 highest ranked ETFs, before inspecting next-month returns. Pair label is y=log(next_month_open/entry_open, first)-log(next_month_open/entry_open, second); only mature valid Open pairs enter training. Pair feature vector is a signed difference of contemporaneous RETURN, ORIGINAL_SMOOTH, mom21, mom63 and logvol63 (all last completed Close at signal_date, NEVER event or next-month prices). Do not include ticker ID, month/year or later winners. To enforce pair-antisymmetry, every training pair is included with both orientations (X,y)/(−X,−y), with sign label flipped and fit_intercept=False. Pair missing features are zero-filled after retaining raw missingness separately in QA, using only asof values. StandardScaler fit train-only, no dimensionality reduction. Pooled Top10 pairs are NOT independent: weight observations so total weight in each historical monthly group is equal, with both orientations summed to 1 per month. Full training eligibility cutoffs: only month pos<=prediction pos−2, and min 24 mature months. One pair learner per market per event, for historical rolling dates 2021–2026, no train/test grouping overlap.

Two fixed arms with no hyperparameter search:
- TOP10_RIDGE: Ridge(alpha=20,fit_intercept=False), predict signed next-month logspread for actual OLC19-context Top2; w1=clip(.5+.25*tanh(pred_mu/train_month_spread_sd),.25,.75); sigma fit from mature Top10 pair labels only, floor 1e-5. Fallback 50/50 until enough training.
- TOP10_LOGIT: LogisticRegression(C=.1,fit_intercept=False,solver='lbfgs',max_iter=1000). Predict P(ETF1 outperforms ETF2), w1=.25+.5*p. Fallback 50/50.
Both are additive continuous allocations, not a new selection/risk policy.

Evaluation before portfolio: out-of-year prequential MSE/Brier and sign Spearman for selected pair vs mean-only/empirical win prior; compare OLC74A same cohorts and 2024/2025/2026, time-block bootstrap 6 mo B=4000 seed74009. Then exactly 2 × 2 timing × 2 markets × 2 fees = 16 additional OLC19 replays with original FAST_LOCK3, V6 and fees .001/.003. Parity of the original OLC74A 7 policies already checked; new runner shares exact engine and event keys. Promotion gate still requires beating original 50/50 in both EU and USA MAIN and FULL, FULL MaxDD no worse than 3pp, demonstrable MAIN predictive improvement, no concentration in 1–2 months; otherwise stop. All historical dates are premined; any historical peak is descriptive only.

Limitations: EU original observed mask available, USA original observed mask not; ticker outcomes dependent within monthly group; no actual causality identified from correlations, only deterministic simulator policy intervention with fixed prices/market impact assumption.
