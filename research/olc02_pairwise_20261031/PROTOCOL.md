## Question and restricted actions
OLC01 predicted absolute ETF returns and allowed selection/cash, leading to wrong exits and retained losers. OLC02 predicts the DIFFERENCE between a candidate primary-sleeve portfolio and its actual incumbent, conditional on the CL01 proposal and known exposures. It is not another independent ranking of all ETF returns.

At each existing monthly event compare HOLD with accepting the current CL01 Top2 at relative weights50/50. A separately identified extension permits replacement of only one incumbent slot by a current CL01 Top2 name, still at50/50. No unrelated ticker may be bought; no new discretionary cash, exposure reduction or position concentration. If stops leave fewer than two actual primary holdings, follow the original CL01 entry/reentry rule rather than label absent holdings a trained HOLD decision. Risk controls remain able to sell or reduce exposure. Before2021 and when required models/features are unavailable use the declared CL01 proposal fallback, logged explicitly.

HOLD preserves the current desired pair and weights; the original independent risk/reentry machinery can still operate. It does not mean zero future orders or a permanently frozen portfolio. Source engine is reused byte-for-byte; new callback reads only prior-close state, never today's Open/Low/gap.
