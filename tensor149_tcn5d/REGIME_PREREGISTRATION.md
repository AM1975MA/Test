## Hypothesis
Relative performance of Original149 MOM21, TCN(B_DAILY_BASE) and XGBoost125(E_XGB_BASE) may depend on market regime detectable at close(t). Strictly compare a spectral RMT-informed HMM gate against simple regime features, non-HMM regime buckets, same-architecture trailing performance and equal-weight ensemble. A backtest winner by year is not evidence of time-t regime prediction.

## Regimes
As-of close(t) 126 daily close-to-close returns from contemporaneously eligible complete-history ETFs, minimum 60, no future ffill. BASIC state variables: SPY mom21, SPY mom63, SPY vol21, ETF universe breadth mom21, cross-sectional dispersion mom21, mean ETF return correlation126. SPECTRAL additional: λmax/trace(correlation), exp(entropy of eigenvalues)/N effective spectral rank, λmax divided by iid Marchenko–Pastur bound. RMT iid theoretical edge heuristic only; ETF dependencies and N/T≈1 invalidate a simplistic hard denoise threshold. Compare 3-state diagonal Gaussian HMM on BASIC and SPECTRAL features with annual January refits on pre-year signals ONLY, historical standardization, forward-only Hamilton filter (never backward-smoothed). Simpler comparator uses prior-year fitted median SPY volatility and mean correlation 4-bucket discretization; past/current regime matches only.

## Causal meta weights fixed in advance
Expert scores rank transformed within date to percentile (-.5,.5). Ensemble STATIC_EQUAL scores are average of three expert ranks. TRAILING_ONLY assigns estimated expected expert nominal 5-day basket returns from last 104 matured weekly predictions. BASIC_HMM / RMT_HMM shrink regime-local nominal return mean towards unconditional trailing mean, using regime similarity posterior dot products and pseudo count 24. SIMPLE_BUCKET uses same weighting with volatility-correlation regime exact match. All adaptive strategies use the SAME (and preregistered) mapping: 50% static equal prior plus 50% softmax of predicted expert return deviations divided by fixed 1% temperature. Sum weights exactly one, no cash/leveraging; same common eligibility set and transaction costs. HMM states are exchangeable and need not have labels such as risk-on permanently assigned across annual refits.

## What constitutes success?
Incremental 2020-2026 forward RankIC, Top5 actual oracle hit rate, NAV CAGR, MaxDD, Sharpe, weekly returns and year-by-year stability. Most importantly, same-date paired 5-week circular-block bootstrap for RMT_HMM versus STATIC_EQUAL, TRAILING_ONLY, BASIC_HMM, best static frozen expert XGB, and MOM21. Performance and statistical intervals are descriptive; heavily data-mined original ETF roster and 2017–26 prevent claiming independent live superiority. Report and preserve all model performances including failures, no post-hoc temperature tuning. Strongest test: RMT_HMM must beat BASIC_HMM and simple trailing control, not merely weakest standalone model.
